# Referral Request Writer

## Referral Types & Templates

### 1. Close Contact (Friend/Former Colleague)
```
Hey [Name],

Hope you're doing well! I noticed [Company] has an opening for [Role] and
I'm really interested. Since you're there, would you be comfortable
referring me?

Here's my resume: [link/attachment]
Job posting: [link]

Happy to return the favor anytime. Let me know if you'd like to chat first!

Thanks,
[Name]
```

### 2. Professional Connection (Former Manager/Peer)
```
Hi [Name],

I hope things are going well at [Company]. I came across the [Role]
position and immediately thought of you.

I'm currently exploring new opportunities in [performance marketing/paid
media] and believe my experience in [specific area] would be a strong fit
for what [Company] is building. Would you be open to referring me for
the role?

I've attached my resume and here's the job link: [URL]

I'd be happy to jump on a quick call if you'd like to discuss. And of
course, I'm always here if you need anything in return.

Best,
[Name]
```

### 3. Weak Connection (Met Once / LinkedIn Only)
```
Hi [Name],

We connected [at X event / on LinkedIn] and I've enjoyed following your
work at [Company]. I'm reaching out because I noticed an opening for
[Role] that aligns well with my background in [specialty].

I know we haven't spoken in a while, but I'd really appreciate any insight
you could share about the team or role. If you feel comfortable, a referral
would mean a lot.

Here's my resume and the job posting: [links]

Either way, I'd love to reconnect and hear how things are going at [Company].

Best,
[Name]
```

### 4. Referral Thank You (After They Refer You)
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you so much for referring me for the [Role] position at [Company].
I really appreciate you taking the time to put in a good word.

I'll keep you posted on how it goes. If there's ever anything I can do
to return the favor, please don't hesitate to ask.

Thanks again!
[Name]
```

## Referral Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Make it easy (provide resume + job link) | Ask someone you barely know without context |
| Give them an out ("no worries if not") | Pressure them |
| Follow up on the outcome | Ghost them after getting referred |
| Thank them regardless of outcome | Only reach out when you need something |
| Offer to reciprocate | Assume they'll do it |

## Rules
- Always personalize — never copy-paste without customization
- Include your resume and the specific job link
- Give them an easy way to say no
- Follow up with results (they invested social capital for you)
- Build referral relationships before you need them
