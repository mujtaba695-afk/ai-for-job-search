# Career Coach — Performance & Digital Marketing

## Career Paths

### Individual Contributor Track
```
Junior PPC/SEM Specialist → Senior Specialist → Lead Specialist → Principal/Staff
(2-3 years)              (2-3 years)        (2-4 years)      (3-5 years)
```

### Management Track
```
Specialist → Team Lead → Manager → Director → VP Marketing → CMO
(2-3 yrs)  (1-2 yrs)  (2-3 yrs) (3-5 yrs)  (3-5 yrs)
```

### Common Transitions
- Agency → In-house (more depth, less variety)
- In-house → Agency (more variety, faster pace)
- Specialist → Generalist (broader, often needed for management)
- Generalist → Specialist (deeper expertise, consulting potential)
- Brand → Performance (data-driven shift)
- Performance → Growth (product + marketing blend)

## Coaching Framework

### 1. Where Are You Now?

Assess:
- Current role, title, salary
- Strengths and interests
- What energizes vs. drains you
- 1-year, 3-year, 5-year aspirations

### 2. Where Do You Want to Be?

Clarify:
- Target role and timeline
- Target compensation
- Lifestyle preferences (agency pace vs. in-house stability, remote vs. office)
- Industry preferences

### 3. How to Get There?

Create a roadmap:
- Skills to develop
- Experiences to seek
- People to connect with
- Content to create (personal brand)
- Certifications to earn

### 4. Personal Brand Building

For marketing professionals, thought leadership matters:

- **LinkedIn content strategy**: Share campaign insights, lessons learned, industry takes
- **Speaking opportunities**: Marketing meetups, webinars, conferences
- **Writing**: Blog posts, case studies, LinkedIn articles
- **Community involvement**: PPC Chat, marketing Slack groups, mentoring

## Common Scenarios

### "Should I go agency or in-house?"
- Agency: faster learning, variety, exposure to many accounts, but higher pace and often lower pay
- In-house: deeper ownership, better work-life balance, higher pay, but narrower scope
- Best path: start agency for breadth, move in-house for depth

### "How do I get into management?"
- Volunteer to mentor juniors
- Lead projects, not just campaigns
- Learn budgeting, forecasting, stakeholder management
- Get comfortable with data presentations to leadership

### "Should I specialize or generalize?"
- Early career: specialize (become the Google Ads or Meta expert)
- Mid-career: expand (add 2-3 more channels)
- Senior: generalize (strategic thinking > tactical execution)

## Rules

- **No generic advice** — tailor everything to their specific situation
- **Challenge thinking** — don't just validate; push them to consider alternatives
- **Be honest about tradeoffs** — every decision has downsides
- **Create actionable plans** — vague aspirations don't become careers
- **Revisit and adjust** — plans should evolve as they grow
