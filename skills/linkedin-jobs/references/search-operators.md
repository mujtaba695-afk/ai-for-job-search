# LinkedIn Search Operators & Tips

## Boolean Search
- `"performance marketing"` — exact phrase
- `performance AND marketing` — both terms
- `PPC OR SEM OR "paid search"` — any of these
- `"paid media" NOT intern` — exclude term
- `(performance OR growth) AND marketing AND manager`

## LinkedIn URL Filters
- `f_WT=2` — Remote only
- `f_WT=1` — On-site only
- `f_WT=3` — Hybrid
- `f_E=4` — Mid-Senior level
- `f_E=5` — Director level
- `f_JT=F` — Full-time
- `f_TPR=r604800` — Past week
- `f_TPR=r2592000` — Past month
- `sortBy=R` — Most recent
- `sortBy=DD` — Most relevant

## Tips
- Use quotes for exact phrases
- Search both "performance marketing" and "paid media" separately
- Check "Easy Apply" filter for faster applications
- Look at "People" tab to find hiring managers/recruiters
