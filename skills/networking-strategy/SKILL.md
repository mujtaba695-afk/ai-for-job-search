# Networking Strategy — Performance & Digital Marketing

## Outreach Types

### 1. LinkedIn Connection Request (300 chars max)
```
Hi [Name], I'm a [your role] specializing in [paid media/PPC/etc.]. I've been following
[Company]'s work on [specific campaign/achievement] and would love to connect. Looking forward
to learning from your experience in [specific area].
```

### 2. LinkedIn Direct Message (after connecting)
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]! I noticed you're doing impressive work at [Company] —
particularly [specific thing you noticed]. I'm exploring [performance marketing/paid media]
roles and would love to hear about your experience at [Company]. Would you be open to a
quick 15-minute chat? Happy to work around your schedule.
```

### 3. Cold Email to Hiring Manager
```
Subject: [Role Title] — [Your Name] | [One compelling metric]

Hi [Name],

I came across the [Role] opening at [Company] and was impressed by [specific company
achievement/campaign]. With [X years] in [performance marketing/paid media] — most recently
[brief achievement with metric] — I believe I can help [specific contribution to their goals].

I've attached my resume and would welcome the chance to discuss how my experience in
[specific area] aligns with what you're building at [Company].

Would you have 15 minutes this week for a brief conversation?

Best,
[Name]
```

### 4. Follow-Up After No Response (5-7 days later)
```
Hi [Name], wanted to circle back on my message about the [Role] position. I recently
[shared a relevant insight/achieved a result] that I thought might be relevant to [Company]'s
[current initiative]. Happy to share more if you're interested.
```

### 5. Informational Interview Request
```
Hi [Name], I'm a [role] with [X years] in [specialty]. I'm researching [Company/industry]
and your career path really resonates with me — especially [specific thing]. Would you be
open to a 20-minute informational interview? I'd love to learn about [specific topic].
```

## Networking Channels

| Channel | Best For | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Recruiters, hiring managers, peers | Connection + message |
| Email | Hiring managers, executives | Cold outreach |
| Twitter/X | Industry thought leaders | Engage with content first |
| Marketing events | Broad networking | In-person, follow up within 24h |
| Slack communities | Peer networking | Be helpful first, ask later |
| Industry conferences | Senior connections | Attend sessions, ask questions |

## Rules

- **Personalize everything** — no copy-paste templates without customization
- **Give before you ask** — share insights, comment on their posts, be helpful
- **Be specific** — "I admire your work" is weak; "Your Meta Ads case study on scaling DTC brands was eye-opening" is strong
- **Respect their time** — ask for 15-20 minutes, not an hour
- **Follow up, don't stalk** — one follow-up is fine, two is the max
