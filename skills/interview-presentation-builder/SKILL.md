# Interview Presentation Builder

## Common Presentation Types

### 1. "Present a Campaign" (Most Common)

```
Slide 1: Title — Campaign Name, Your Role, Result
Slide 2: The Challenge — Business problem, constraints
Slide 3: The Strategy — Your approach, why you chose it
Slide 4: The Execution — Tactics, audiences, creatives, budget allocation
Slide 5: The Results — Key metrics (charts/tables)
Slide 6: Key Learnings — What worked, what didn't, what you'd change
Slide 7: Q&A — Open for discussion
```

### 2. "First 90 Days" Plan

```
Slide 1: Title — Your Name, Role, "First 90 Days"
Slide 2: Understanding — How you'll learn the business, accounts, team
Slide 3: Quick Wins (Days 1-30) — Audit, identify low-hanging fruit
Slide 4: Strategic Initiatives (Days 31-60) — Major projects to start
Slide 5: Scale & Optimize (Days 61-90) — Growth plan
Slide 6: KPIs & Reporting — How you'll measure success
Slide 7: Questions
```

### 3. Strategy Pitch (Agency/Director Level)

```
Slide 1: Title — "[Company] Paid Media Strategy"
Slide 2: Current State — Audit findings, gaps, opportunities
Slide 3: Target Audience — Personas, segments, insights
Slide 4: Channel Strategy — Mix, budget allocation, rationale
Slide 5: Creative Strategy — Messaging, formats, testing plan
Slide 6: Measurement — Attribution, KPIs, reporting framework
Slide 7: Timeline & Milestones
Slide 8: Expected Outcomes — Projections, scenarios
Slide 9: Q&A
```

## Design Guidelines

- **Clean and minimal** — no clutter
- **Data visualization** — charts > tables for metrics
- **Consistent branding** — same colors/fonts throughout
- **6 words per bullet max** — talk through details, don't read slides
- **One idea per slide**
- **Company colors** if known — shows you researched them

## Output

Generate a PowerPoint file using the wps-ppt-generator skill, or provide slide-by-slide content in markdown for the user to build themselves.

## Rules
- Always customize for the specific company and role
- Include real numbers (even if approximated)
- Practice the timing (usually 15-20 minutes + Q&A)
- Prepare for tough questions after each section
- Have a backup version with more detail (in case they want to go deep)
