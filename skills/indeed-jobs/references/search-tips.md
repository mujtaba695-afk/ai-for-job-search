# Indeed Search Tips for Marketing Roles

## Advanced Operators
- `"performance marketing"` — exact phrase
- `title:performance marketing` — keyword in title only
- `title:(performance OR "paid media" OR growth)` — multiple title keywords
- `$60,000` — minimum salary filter
- `fromage:3` — posted in last 3 days
- `fromage:7` — posted in last week

## Indeed-Specific Features
- **Salary estimates** — Indeed calculates estimated salary for most roles
- **Company reviews** — Read before applying
- **Indeed Assessments** — Some employers require skill tests
- **Resume upload** — Allows recruiters to find you
- **Job alerts** — Can set up email alerts for specific searches

## Tips
- Indeed often has roles from smaller companies not on LinkedIn
- Use "date posted" filter to avoid stale listings
- Check "Salary Estimate" filter to target your range
- Company pages show other open roles (good for cold applications)
