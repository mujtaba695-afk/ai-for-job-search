# Portfolio Builder — Performance & Digital Marketing

## Workflow

### 1. Collect Campaign Data

Ask user for:
- Campaign name/client/brand
- Channel(s) used (Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, etc.)
- Objective (awareness, conversions, app installs, etc.)
- Budget (total and monthly)
- Duration
- Results (ROAS, CPA, revenue, conversions, CTR, etc.)
- Screenshots (dashboards, ads, creatives — optional)
- Challenges faced and how they were solved

### 2. Structure Each Case Study

```
## [Campaign Name] — [Channel] | [Industry]

### The Challenge
[2-3 sentences: What was the problem? What were the constraints?]

### The Strategy
[3-4 sentences: What approach did you take? Why? What was the targeting,
creative, bidding, and funnel strategy?]

### The Execution
- [Key tactical decisions]
- [A/B tests run]
- [Audience segments created]
- [Creative variations tested]

### The Results
| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROAS | 2.1x | 4.3x | +105% |
| CPA | $45 | $22 | -51% |
| Monthly Revenue | $50K | $120K | +140% |

### Key Takeaways
- [What you learned]
- [What you'd do differently]
- [Scalability insights]
```

### 3. Portfolio Formats

**PDF Portfolio** — Professional, downloadable, interview-ready
**Personal Website** — HTML/CSS single-page (generate code)
**Notion/Google Docs** — Easy to share and update
**Slide Deck** — For presentations (use PowerPoint skill)

### 4. Output

Generate polished case study documents. For website format, create a clean single-page HTML with:
- Hero section with name and title
- Case study cards with key metrics
- Skills/platforms section
- Contact/CTA

## Rules

- **Metrics are king** — every case study needs numbers
- **Tell a story** — challenge → strategy → execution → results
- **Be specific** — "Optimized Meta Ads" is weak; "Restructured Meta Ads account from 3 ad sets to 15 lookalike segments" is strong
- **Honest but polished** — don't fabricate, but do frame results in the best light
- **Visual if possible** — ask for screenshots, mock up dashboards
