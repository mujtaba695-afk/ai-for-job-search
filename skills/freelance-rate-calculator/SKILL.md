# Freelance Rate Calculator — Marketing Roles

## Rate Calculation

### Step 1: Determine Base Rate

Research market rates for the specific role and experience level:

```
Role: [Performance Marketing Manager]
Experience: [5 years]
Location: [US/UK/Remote]
Market Rate Range: $[X] - $[Y] /hour
```

### Step 2: Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) Conversion

```
Annual FTE Salary: $120,000
÷ Working days: 260
÷ Billable hours per day: 7 (non-billable: meetings, admin)
= Daily rate: $462
= Hourly rate: $66

Freelance premium (no benefits, no job security): +30-50%
= Freelance hourly: $86 - $99
= Freelance daily: $600 - $700
```

### Step 3: Freelance-Specific Costs

Add these to your rate:
```
| Cost | Monthly | Annual | Per Hour (1,400 hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-employment tax | - | +$X | +$X |
| Health insurance | $500 | $6,000 | $4.29 |
| Retirement (no match) | $500 | $6,000 | $4.29 |
| Equipment/software | $200 | $2,400 | $1.71 |
| Unpaid vacation (4 wks) | - | $4,615 | $3.30 |
| Business insurance | $100 | $1,200 | $0.86 |
| **Total add-on** | | **$20,215** | **$14.44** |
```

### Step 4: Final Rate

```
Base hourly rate: $66
Freelance premium (+40%): $92
Cost add-on: $14
Minimum viable rate: $106/hour

Recommended rate: $120-150/hour
Premium rate (specialized/urgency): $175-200/hour
```

### Project-Based Pricing

For fixed-scope projects:
```
Campaign Audit: $1,500 - $5,000
Account Setup (Google Ads): $2,000 - $5,000
Monthly Retainer (management): $2,000 - $10,000/mo
Strategy Document: $3,000 - $8,000
Training/Workshop: $2,000 - $5,000/day
```

## Rules
- Never quote hourly for project work — use project-based pricing
- Always ask about scope before quoting
- Factor in revision rounds and meetings
- Higher rates for rush jobs (+25-50%)
- Retainers are better than hourly for ongoing work
