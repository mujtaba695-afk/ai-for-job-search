## Exam Tips
- Google exams: 80% pass rate, retake after 24 hours if failed
- Meta exams: Proctored, harder, practice with their study guides
- Use practice tests before the real exam
- Screenshots aren't allowed — study key metrics and definitions

## Rules
- Prioritize free certifications (Google, Meta, TikTok)
- Stack certifications strategically (don't get 5 Google Ads certs, diversify)
- Certifications don't replace experience but they open doors
- Put certifications on LinkedIn immediately after passing
- Renew before expiry (most are annual)
