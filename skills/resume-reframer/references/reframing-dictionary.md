# Reframing Dictionary — Marketing Experience

## Common Reframes (Honest)

### Entry-Level / Junior Experience

| What You Did | How to Frame It |
|---|---|
| Posted on social media | Managed social media content creation and publishing |
| Answered customer DMs | Handled customer engagement and community management |
| Made graphics in Canva | Created marketing collateral and ad creative assets |
| Helped with email blasts | Supported email marketing campaign execution |
| Took notes in meetings | Contributed to cross-functional marketing strategy discussions |
| Did competitor research | Conducted competitive analysis to inform marketing strategy |
| Updated the website | Managed website content updates and optimization |
| Ran reports in Google Analytics | Generated performance reports using Google Analytics |
| Helped set up ad account | Assisted with advertising platform setup and configuration |

### Mid-Level Experience

| What You Did | How to Frame It |
|---|---|
| Managed Facebook ads | Led paid social advertising campaigns on Meta platform |
| Ran Google Ads | Managed search and display advertising through Google Ads |
| Did keyword research | Conducted keyword research and search strategy development |
| Made monthly reports | Developed monthly performance dashboards and ROI analysis |
| Tested different ads | Designed and executed A/B testing frameworks for creative optimization |
| Worked with influencers | Managed influencer partnership and collaboration programs |
| Handled the marketing budget | Oversaw marketing budget allocation and spend optimization |
| Trained new team members | Led onboarding and training for marketing team members |
| Worked with sales team | Collaborated with sales on lead generation and conversion optimization |

### Senior-Level Experience

| What You Did | How to Frame It |
|---|---|
| Built the ad strategy | Developed comprehensive paid media strategy across channels |
| Grew the team | Scaled marketing team from X to Y, establishing processes and workflows |
| Reported to CEO | Presented marketing performance and strategic recommendations to executive leadership |
| Managed agency relationships | Directed external agency partnerships, ensuring alignment with business objectives |
| Launched new channels | Expanded marketing mix by launching and scaling new acquisition channels |
| Reduced CPA | Optimized acquisition cost efficiency through systematic testing and refinement |
| Scaled campaigns | Drove scalable growth while maintaining efficiency targets |

| Ran | Managed / Directed / Oversaw |
| Was responsible for | Owned / Drove / Delivered |
| Assisted | Partnered / Collaborated / Enabled |
| Tried | Tested / Validated / Experimented |
