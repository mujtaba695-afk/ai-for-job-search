### 3. Career Trajectory Analysis

For each offer, project:
- **1 year**: What will you have learned? What will your resume look like?
- **3 years**: Promotion potential? Skill growth? Network expansion?
- **5 years**: Does this role set you up for Director/VP?

### 4. Red Flags in Offers

| Signal | Concern |
|---|---|
| Lowball offer (>20% below market) | They don't value the role |
| "We'll review salary in 6 months" | Empty promise |
| No equity in a startup | They don't want you as an owner |
| Vague bonus structure | Bonus may never materialize |
| Unlimited PTO + no one takes it | Culture problem |
| Offer expires in 24 hours | Pressure tactic |

## Rules
- Always calculate TOTAL compensation, not just base salary
- Factor in career trajectory, not just immediate pay
- Consider the manager/team quality from interview impressions
- Don't let excitement override rational evaluation
- Always negotiate unless the offer exceeds your expectations
