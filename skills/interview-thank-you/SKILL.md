# Interview Thank You Writer

## Timing
Send within **2-4 hours** of the interview. Max 24 hours.

## Template Structure

### Standard Thank You (to Hiring Manager)
```
Subject: Thank you — [Role Title] interview

Hi [Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the [Role] position.
I enjoyed learning about [specific thing discussed — their Q4 campaign plan,
team structure, growth goals, etc.].

Our conversation reinforced my excitement about this opportunity. Particularly,
[reference 1-2 specific discussion points and connect them to your experience]:

- [Point 1]: "When we discussed [topic], it reminded me of how I [relevant
  experience with metric]."
- [Point 2]: "I'm confident my experience in [area] would help [Company]
  achieve [their stated goal]."

If you have any follow-up questions or need additional information, please
don't hesitate to reach out. I look forward to hearing about next steps.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

### Multi-Interviewer Thank You
Send personalized versions to each interviewer. Reference something unique from each conversation.

### Addressing Concerns
If a gap was raised during the interview:
```
I've been thinking about your question regarding [topic]. While my direct
experience in [specific area] is [honest assessment], I believe my [related
experience] translates directly because [explain connection]. I'm also
[action you're taking to close the gap].
```

### Short Version (for quick follow-up)
```
Hi [Name], thanks for a great conversation today. I'm even more excited about
the [Role] after learning about [specific thing]. Happy to provide any
additional info. Looking forward to next steps!
```

## Rules
- Reference SPECIFIC conversation points (generic thank-yous are worthless)
- Keep it under 200 words
- Don't be desperate or overly enthusiastic
- Proofread twice — typos in a follow-up are career-ending
- If they said "we'll be in touch by Friday," don't follow up before Monday
