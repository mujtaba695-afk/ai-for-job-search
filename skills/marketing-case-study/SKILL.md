# Marketing Case Study Builder

## Case Study Framework

### Structure (The IMPACT Method)

```
## [Campaign Name] — [Channel] | [Industry]

### I — Initial Situation
- What was the business challenge?
- What was the current state (baseline metrics)?
- What constraints existed (budget, time, resources)?

### M — Mission (Objective)
- What was the specific goal?
- What KPIs were set?
- What was the timeline?

### P — Plan (Strategy)
- What was the overall approach?
- Why this strategy over alternatives?
- What was the target audience?
- What channels/tactics were chosen?

### A — Action (Execution)
- Step-by-step implementation
- A/B tests run and decisions made
- Budget allocation across channels
- Creative/audience/bidding decisions
- Tools and platforms used

### C — Consequences (Results)
| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| ROAS | 2.1x | 4.3x | +105% |
| CPA | $45 | $22 | -51% |
| Revenue | $50K/mo | $120K/mo | +140% |
| Conversions | 200/mo | 520/mo | +160% |

### T — Takeaways
- Key learnings
- What would you do differently?
- How this applies to future campaigns
```

### Interview-Ready Version (2 minutes)

Condense to:
1. **Situation** (15 sec): The problem
2. **Strategy** (30 sec): Your approach
3. **Execution** (30 sec): Key decisions
4. **Results** (30 sec): The numbers
5. **Learning** (15 sec): What you took away

### Presentation Version
- 5-slide deck: Problem → Strategy → Execution → Results → Learnings
- Use charts/graphs for metrics
- Include screenshots if available

## Rules
- Every case study MUST have quantified results
- Start with your BEST campaign (highest ROAS, biggest scale)
- Be specific about YOUR contribution (not team's)
- Include budget scale to show scope
- If exact numbers are confidential, use percentages or ranges
