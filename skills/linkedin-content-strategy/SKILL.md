# LinkedIn Content Strategy — Marketing Professional

## Content Pillars

### 1. Campaign Insights (40% of posts)
Share real learnings from your work:
```
"Last month I tested broad targeting on Meta for a DTC brand.

Everyone said it was dead.

Results:
→ CPA dropped 32%
→ ROAS went from 2.8x to 4.1x
→ Scale went from $5K/day to $15K/day

Here's what I learned about why broad targeting works in 2025..."
```

### 2. Industry Commentary (25%)
Share takes on marketing news/trends:
```
"Google just announced [feature].

Here's what this actually means for performance marketers:

1. [Implication 1]
2. [Implication 2]
3. [What to do about it]

Hot take: This is actually good for us because..."
```

### 3. Career Lessons (20%)
Share professional growth insights:
```
"5 years ago I was a junior PPC specialist making $45K.

Today I'm a Performance Marketing Director managing $2M/month in spend.

Here are 5 things I wish I knew earlier..."
```

### 4. Engagement Posts (15%)
Ask questions, polls, controversial takes:
```
"Unpopular opinion: ROAS is a terrible metric for measuring marketing success.

Here's why...

What metric do you think matters most?"
```

## Content Calendar (Weekly)

| Day | Content Type | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Campaign insight or lesson | 8:00 AM |
| Wednesday | Industry take or hot take | 12:00 PM |
| Friday | Career lesson or engagement post | 5:00 PM |

## Growth Tactics

1. **Comment on 10 posts daily** from marketing leaders
2. **Reply to every comment** on your own posts
3. **Use 3-5 relevant hashtags** (not more)
4. **Tag people** when referencing their work (with permission)
5. **Post consistently** — same days, same times
6. **No external links in posts** (kills reach) — put in comments

## Rules
- Be authentic — share real experiences, not generic advice
- Numbers get attention — always include metrics
- Controversial takes drive engagement (but stay professional)
- Consistency > virality — show up regularly
- Never badmouth current/former employers
