# Remote Job Boards — Complete Reference

## Tier 1: Remote-Native (Best Quality)

### We Work Remotely (weworkremotely.com)
- Largest remote-only job board
- Categories: Programming, Design, Marketing, Customer Support, etc.
- No account needed to browse
- Free for job seekers

### Remotive (remotive.com)
- Curated remote jobs
- Strong in tech and marketing
- Community features (Slack, events)
- Newsletter with top picks

### FlexJobs (flexjobs.com)
- Vetted, scam-free listings
- Hand-screened jobs
- Paid subscription ($24.95/month or $59.95/year)
- Best for serious job seekers

### Remote.co (remote.co)
- Curated remote listings
- Company profiles and remote culture info
- Job categories well-organized
- Free for job seekers

### JustRemote (justremote.org)
- Clean, simple interface
- Global remote jobs
- No account needed
- Free

### Working Nomads (workingnomads.com)
- Curated for digital nomads
- Categories: Development, Marketing, Design, etc.
- Daily email digest
- Free

### Himalayas (himalayas.app)
- Transparent company profiles
- Shows salary, tech stack, team size
- Great for researching remote companies
- Free

### Remote OK (remoteok.com)
- Aggregator — pulls from many sources
- Very high volume
- Can be noisy — filter carefully
- Free

## Tier 2: Major Boards with Remote Filters

### LinkedIn
- Use Remote filter + performance marketing keywords
- Largest volume but quality varies
- Easy Apply for many roles

### Indeed
- Add "remote" to any search
- Good for finding roles at traditional companies going remote

### AngelList/Wellfound
- Best for startup remote roles
- Shows equity, salary, company stage
- Apply directly

### Otta
- Tech-focused, good remote filter
- Shows company culture scores
- European and US companies

## Tier 3: Niche Remote Marketing

### MarketingHire
- Marketing-specific job board
- Has remote filter
- Quality listings from real companies

### Jobspresso
- Curated remote jobs
- Marketing, customer support, tech
- Free

### PowerToFly
- Diversity and inclusion focused
- Remote marketing roles
- Events and community

### Authentic Jobs
- Design and marketing
- Quality companies
- Has remote filter

## Tips for Remote Job Search

1. **Search multiple boards** — Remote jobs are scattered
2. **Check company career pages** — Many remote-first companies only post on their own sites
3. **Follow remote-first companies** — GitLab, Automattic, Buffer, Zapier, Doist, etc.
4. **Set up alerts** — Remote jobs get filled fast
5. **Tailor resume for remote** — Highlight async communication, self-management, remote tools experience
