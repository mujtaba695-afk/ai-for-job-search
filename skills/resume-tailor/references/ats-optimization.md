# ATS Optimization Guide — Marketing Resumes

## ATS-Friendly Formatting Rules

1. **No tables** — ATS parsers break on table layouts
2. **No columns** — Single-column text only
3. **No graphics/icons** — Pure text
4. **Standard headings**: "Professional Summary", "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills", "Certifications"
5. **No headers/footers** — Some ATS can't read them
6. **File format**: .docx is safest; .pdf works for most modern ATS
7. **Standard fonts**: Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Helvetica
8. **No abbreviations without full form first**: "Search Engine Marketing (SEM)" then use "SEM" after

## Keyword Density Targets

- **Primary keywords** (exact match from JD): appear 2-3 times across resume
- **Secondary keywords**: appear at least once
- **Tools/platforms**: listed in dedicated Skills section AND demonstrated in experience bullets

## Power Verbs for Marketing Resumes

### Strategy & Leadership
Owned, Drove, Spearheaded, Directed, Orchestrated, Championed, Led, Managed

### Optimization & Analysis
Optimized, Analyzed, Measured, Tracked, Attributed, Tested, Iterated, Diagnosed

### Growth & Scaling
Scaled, Grew, Expanded, Increased, Accelerated, Maximized, Amplified

### Budget & ROI
Managed ($X budget), Allocated, Reduced (CPA by X%), Improved (ROAS by X%), Delivered ($X revenue)

### Technical Execution
Launched, Built, Configured, Automated, Implemented, Integrated, Migrated

## Quantification Cheat Sheet

Always add numbers. If user doesn't provide exact figures, ask them to estimate:

- **Budget managed**: "Managed $2M+ annual paid media budget across 5 channels"
- **ROAS/ROI**: "Achieved 4.5x ROAS on Meta Ads, exceeding target by 25%"
- **CPA reduction**: "Reduced CPA from $45 to $28 through A/B testing and audience refinement"
- **Revenue driven**: "Drove $3.2M in attributed revenue through Google Ads campaigns"
- **Scale**: "Scaled monthly spend from $50K to $200K while maintaining efficiency targets"
- **Team**: "Led team of 4 specialists managing 15+ active campaigns"
- **Time**: "Reduced campaign launch time by 40% through automated workflows"
- **Conversion rate**: "Improved landing page CVR from 2.1% to 3.8%"

## Common ATS Systems (and quirks)

- **Greenhouse**: Good at parsing; prefers .docx
- **Lever**: Similar to Greenhouse; handles .pdf well
- **Workday**: Can be strict; use standard headings
- **Taleo**: Older; avoid complex formatting
- **iCIMS**: Handles both formats well
- **LinkedIn EasyApply**: Simple text extraction; keep it clean
