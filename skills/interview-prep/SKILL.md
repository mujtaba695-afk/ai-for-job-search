# Interview Prep — Performance & Digital Marketing

## Interview Types Covered

1. **Phone Screen / Recruiter Call** — Culture fit, basic experience
2. **Technical / Marketing Deep-Dive** — Platform knowledge, campaign strategy
3. **Case Study / Whiteboard** — Live problem-solving
4. **Hiring Manager** — Leadership, vision, team fit
5. **Panel / Cross-Functional** — Collaboration, communication
6. **Executive / Final Round** — Strategic thinking, company alignment

## Question Bank by Category

### Technical — Platform Knowledge
- Walk me through how you'd structure a Google Ads account for [scenario]
- How do you approach bidding strategies for a new campaign?
- Explain the Meta Ads auction — how does it work?
- How do you set up conversion tracking across platforms?
- What's your approach to keyword research for a new product?
- How do you handle Quality Score optimization?
- Explain the difference between tCPA, tROAS, and Maximize Conversions

### Technical — Strategy & Optimization
- Your CPA increased 30% month-over-month. Walk me through your diagnosis
- How would you allocate a $100K monthly budget across channels?
- How do you approach audience segmentation for paid campaigns?
- What's your A/B testing framework?
- How do you attribute conversions across touchpoints?
- How do you decide when to scale vs. cut a campaign?

### Technical — Analytics & Reporting
- What metrics do you report on weekly vs. monthly?
- How do you build a performance dashboard?
- Explain incrementality testing
- How do you measure brand awareness campaigns?
- What's your approach to ROAS forecasting?

### Behavioral (STAR Format)
- Tell me about a campaign that failed. What did you learn?
- Describe a time you had to push back on a stakeholder's request
- How do you prioritize when managing multiple campaigns/channels?
- Tell me about a time you identified an opportunity others missed
- Describe a cross-functional project you led

### Role-Specific
- **Manager/Lead**: How do you develop team members? How do you handle underperformance?
- **Director/VP**: How do you build a paid media strategy from scratch? How do you present to C-suite?
- **Agency**: How do you manage client expectations? How do you handle scope creep?

## Mock Interview Flow

1. Ask user which interview type to simulate
2. Ask 3-5 questions one at a time
3. After each answer, provide:
   - What was strong
   - What to improve
   - A sample "gold standard" answer
4. End with overall feedback and priority areas

## Answer Frameworks

**STAR**: Situation → Task → Action → Result (for behavioral)
**CAR**: Challenge → Action → Result (for achievements)
**PAR**: Problem → Action → Result (for case studies)

## Rules

- Always ask the user's target role and company to customize questions
- Include questions specific to their target company/industry
- Provide sample answers in the user's voice based on their resume
- Focus on the gap between their current prep level and what's needed
